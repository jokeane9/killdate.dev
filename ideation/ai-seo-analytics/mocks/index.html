Wilder & Co. · AI Visibility

Design space prototypes

Three approaches to displaying product-by-product AI visibility on ChatGPT. Same mock data (5 products × 16 queries across Awareness / Correct Info / Ability to Purchase), same chrome (brand context, navigation, diagnostics sidebar), three genuinely different ways of revealing the data. B&W shadcn aesthetic throughout.

Approach A
·
Recommended for v1

The List

Dense table, one product per row, everything visible on one screen. Section scores as colored pills, sparkline trend, expand-in-place for per-query detail. Scans fast, scales to many products, daily-use surface.

Sweet spot: 10–500 products
Best for: daily ops, fast scanning
Approach B
·
Marketing-friendly

The Cards

Grid of generous cards, one per product. Each card carries product identity with image, headline health score, and three segment-bar progress indicators showing query-level texture. Product-as-subject thinking.

Sweet spot: 5–30 products
Best for: first impression, stakeholder share
Approach C
·
Power-user toggle

The Grid

2D heatmap, products × queries. Patterns visible at a glance — vertical bands reveal systemic query failures, horizontal bands reveal product-specific failures. Diagnostic killer feature, surfaces what no other view can.

Sweet spot: 5–50 products
Best for: pattern recognition, diagnostic work
Built static — Tailwind via CDN, Inter from Google Fonts, inline SVG icons. No build step, opens in any browser.
Mock data is identical across all three approaches. The same Cotton Tee failure pattern shows up differently in each view — observe how.
Reference aesthetic: ui.shadcn.com
